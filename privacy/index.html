Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alki Golf LLC, doing business as DivotLab ("DivotLab," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the DivotLab mobile application and related services (the "Service").

1. Who We Are

DivotLab is a product of Alki Golf LLC. DivotLab provides AI-powered golf swing coaching. Users can sign in, upload golf swing videos, receive AI-generated swing analysis, and ask follow-up coaching questions.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated through your use of the Service, and limited information from service providers that help us operate the Service.

Account information: We may collect your name, email address, sign-in and authentication identifiers, and basic account status.

Swing videos and analysis: We may collect uploaded video files, extracted frames, video metadata, processing status, AI-generated observations and recommendations, follow-up chat context, and analysis history.

Chat and support: We collect messages sent to the AI coach or support. Support requests may include account and app context needed to investigate an issue.

Purchases: Apple processes purchases and RevenueCat manages subscription access. We do not receive full payment-card details. We may receive product, subscription, trial, renewal, cancellation, expiration, customer identifier, and transaction metadata.

Diagnostics: We may collect device platform, app version, logs, crashes, errors, performance data, API request metadata, processing status, and limited feature-usage events. We do not intend to collect precise location, contacts, health records, or biometric identifiers.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create, authenticate, maintain, and protect accounts.
  • Upload, process, and analyze golf swing videos.
  • Generate swing feedback and follow-up coaching.
  • Maintain chat continuity and swing history.
  • Manage trials, subscriptions, restoration, and access.
  • Provide support, debug failures, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.
  • Meet legal, tax, accounting, App Store, and platform obligations.

4. AI Processing

DivotLab uses AI service providers, including OpenAI, to generate swing analysis and conversational coaching. We may send uploaded video frames, extracted observations, user questions, and related swing context to AI systems for processing.

AI-generated feedback may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for a particular person. Do not rely on DivotLab as a substitute for professional coaching, medical advice, physical therapy, or injury-prevention advice.

5. How We Share Information

We may share information with providers that operate the Service, including Amazon Web Services for infrastructure, OpenAI for analysis and chat, RevenueCat for subscription management, Apple for App Store services, and tools used for support. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, prevent fraud or abuse, or complete a corporate transaction.

We do not sell personal information or use uploaded swing videos for third-party advertising.

6. Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. Videos, frames, analyses, chats, and account data may be stored in cloud infrastructure with access limited to systems and personnel who need it to operate, secure, debug, or support the Service.

7. Retention

We retain information as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain accounts and subscriptions, improve reliability, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. We may retain limited records after account deletion when required for security, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, or App Store transaction purposes.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to processing of certain information. You can delete your account and associated swing data from Settings. Contact support@divotlab.ai for other privacy requests.

9. Subscriptions

You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple account. Deleting your DivotLab account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. A higher minimum age may apply where you live.

11. International Processing

We may process and store information in the United States and other locations where we or our service providers operate.

12. Changes

We may update this policy and will provide notice of material changes as appropriate, including by changing the effective date.
