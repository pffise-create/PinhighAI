## Design Context

### Users
Golfers upload swing videos from their phones to get fast, trustworthy coaching. They often review feedback in public or semi-public settings, including on transit, so the experience must work well muted and remain socially comfortable to use.

### Brand Personality
Premium, calm, trustworthy. The coach should feel like a sharp private instructor, not a hype machine or a sports-broadcast caricature.

### Aesthetic Direction
Mobile-first, film-room style coaching with restrained motion graphics. The visual system should feel polished and cinematic without becoming loud, cluttered, or gimmicky. Default to silent-friendly playback with captions visible and audio optional.

### Design Principles
1. Let the swing stay primary; graphics should clarify, not compete.
2. Default to dignity in public use: mute first, captions first, no surprising audio.
3. Make technical analysis feel premium through pacing, composition, and precision rather than visual excess.
4. Keep the interface calm and focused so the user can absorb one coaching point at a time.
5. Ship only experiences that feel coach-grade and deliberate, not templated or AI-shaped.
