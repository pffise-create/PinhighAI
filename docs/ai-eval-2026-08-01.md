# Alki DivotLab — Coaching AI Quality Evaluation
**Date:** 2026-08-01 | **Evaluator:** independent AI-quality agent (read-only on repo; bounded live probes authorized)

## Verdict: PASS-WITH-FIXES

The current pipeline (prompt `2026-02-22.v6` + gpt-5.2 two-stage, deployed today) produces coaching that is grounded, honest, and scoped — a dramatic improvement over the 2025-era outputs still sitting in the table. But it fails a demanding golfer on three counts: **every session sounds the same** (verbatim catchphrases lifted from the prompt's few-shot examples, identical closing question), **it re-diagnoses inconsistently on identical input**, and **it structurally cannot see impact**, so it increasingly punts to "keep doing what you're doing — what's your miss?" A paying user uploading three swings would get three near-identical pep talks.

## Method

- **Part A:** 32 stored production outputs sampled from `golf-coach-analyses` (167 completed records), stratified across 2025-07 → 2026-06. Three eras emerged: legacy (pre-versioned, Aug–Oct 2025, 16 samples), v1–v5 (gpt-4o-mini, Feb 2026, 5 samples), v6 (gpt-5.1/5.2, Feb–Jun 2026, 9 samples).
- **Part B:** 3 completed records with intact S3 frames copied to `eval-20260801-{1,2,3}` under `user_id=eval-agent-user`; `golf-ai-analysis-processor` invoked directly (the real deployed pipeline, model gpt-5.2, pv 2026-02-22.v6). All 3 completed, quality gate passed, no fallback. **All eval records deleted after judging**, including the side-effect profile row the processor wrote for `eval-agent-user` (to `golf-coach-swing-profiles-dev` — see infra note).

## Top 5 failure patterns

### 1. (Legacy — resolved, but still in the table) Meta-leakage and template dumps
Old records leak internals and read like a checklist, e.g. `1756362465924-fzumyc5b2`: *"I see the golfer is wearing a light blue shirt and dark shorts. Here are some key frames to focus on for analysis: 1. **Setup (Frame 1)** ..."* — and `1758430054316-5sc2ht22z` ends a section: *"When you send batch 2, I'll connect these notes to your downswing/impact frames"*, under a header *"Analysis based on 16 frames processed in 2 batches."* `1757130059608-gzh2c4jqo` is a 3,000-char generic golf textbook keyed to frame numbers. `test-swing-123` and `debugloop-1771366730` are bare refusals. These are fixed by v6 — but they're still what a user sees if they scroll back through pre-Feb-2026 history in the app.

### 2. (Current, worst active issue) Prompt few-shot lines parroted verbatim — every session sounds identical
`buildCoachRenderPrompt` (coachingSystemPrompt.js:166) supplies three "good wry style examples." The model copies them near-verbatim:
- **"science project": 9 of 12 v6 outputs** (incl. 2 of 3 live runs) — e.g. `1780460000752`: *"If it doesn't, ditch it—don't turn it into a science project."*; `eval-20260801-2`: *"Don't turn it into a science project."*
- **"swing emergency": 3 of 12** — `1771994954405`: *"nothing here screams 'swing emergency.'"*
- **Identical closer in 12 of 12 v6 outputs**: *"One quick question so I can be specific: what's your typical driver miss—push/slice, pull/hook…?"* (traceable to SYSTEM_PROMPT line 10's parenthetical examples).
- Stock phrasing cluster: "athletic posture/setup" (8/12), "balanced finish" (6/12), "not quitting on it / not hanging back" (5/12).
A returning user hears the same joke and the same closing question every single upload. That is template-speak wearing a wry costume.

### 3. (Current) Run-to-run diagnostic inconsistency on identical frames
Same input, contradictory coaching:
- `1780460000752-rzrv71n1o` (stored, June): diagnosis = backswing too long/high, priority = *"make the top… 5–10% shorter"*, 3/4-backswing drill. **Same frames re-run today** (`eval-20260801-1`): *"a high top tends to work just fine"* — priority = keep everything, smooth transition.
- `1780469517628-gx1qz7jer` (stored): flags hands-ahead/shaft-lean at address as the watch item. Re-run (`eval-20260801-3`): never mentions it; priority becomes "get impact feedback with face spray."
Neither answer is wrong, but a golfer who re-uploads a similar swing gets whiplash — the product contradicts itself about the same swing.

### 4. (Current) Reinforcement bias / diagnostic punt in fresh outputs
All 3 live runs conclude "keep doing what you're doing" and defer the actual diagnosis to the unanswerable closing question. The DIAGNOSTIC live score (2.8) reflects that the current model+prompt, faced with setup/top/finish-only evidence, defaults to affirmation plus a strike-check suggestion. Honest — but a demanding customer paid for a diagnosis, and 3-for-3 "looks solid, what's your miss?" reads as the AI having nothing to say. (The stored v6 set shows the model *can* commit when evidence supports it — `1775714666537` and `1772148200673` are genuinely excellent: specific, causal, one priority.)

### 5. (Structural) Impact is never visible, so the highest-value question is always dodged
Frame extraction samples at 4 fps; the vision-facts stage consistently reports setup/top/finish visible and impact not assessable. Every v6 output contains a variant of *"impact itself isn't clearly visible… face angle, strike location… I can't confirm"* (`1779685166158`, `1779686603814`, `eval-20260801-3`, etc.). The honesty is correct behavior; the pipeline limitation means the product can never address face/path/strike — the thing golfers most want. This caps DIAGNOSTIC at ~4 forever unless frame sampling improves around impact.

## Did today's deployed prompts improve on older outputs?

**Yes, decisively.** Legacy (1.8 mean) → v6 (4.0 stored / 3.7 live). v6 eliminated meta-leakage, refusals, five-swing-thought dumps, and hallucinated specifics (legacy `1755581264951` diagnosed grip strength and "overextension" chains no camera angle supports). v6 outputs cite 2–4 concrete observed positions, keep one priority, and are honest about visibility. The live runs confirm the deployed pipeline matches the best stored v6 quality — with the caveat that today's runs skewed further toward reinforcement (issue 4) and reproduced the catchphrase problem exactly (issue 2).

## Prioritized recommendations

1. **Remove or rotate the verbatim style examples** — `coachingSystemPrompt.js:166` (`buildCoachRenderPrompt`): the three quoted wry lines ("science project", "swing emergency", "good rep") should be replaced with a *description* of the register ("dry, brief, never at the player's expense") or a rotating pool with an explicit instruction: "Never reuse these example phrases verbatim." Same for SYSTEM_PROMPT line 10 — the parenthetical "(typical ball flight, preferred shot shape, strike pattern)" has become a mandatory closer; add "vary the follow-up question and skip it entirely when the priority is clear."
2. **Add an anti-repetition rule fed by history** — `buildDeveloperContext` already passes recent swings and says "Do not imitate or reuse prior coaching wording" (line 79), but the coach-render call is what needs it: pass the previous analysis's key phrases/priority and instruct "do not reuse the previous session's jokes, closer, or priority framing unless deliberately building on it."
3. **Stabilize diagnosis across runs** — the two-stage design already emits `vision_facts` with confidence. Instruct the render stage: "If a prior analysis for this user flagged a pattern that is still visible in the current facts, address it explicitly (confirm improved / unchanged / worse) before introducing a new priority." This converts issue 3 into a continuity feature. (The consolidation prompt at line 105 gestures at this; the render prompt never receives prior priorities.)
4. **Give the reinforcement path more spine** — when the decision is "no change," require one falsifiable checkpoint tied to a cited observation (some outputs do this well — the face-spray check in `eval-20260801-3` is the model at its best) rather than "keep the feel." Add to `buildAnalysisNaturalContractPrompt`: "If you recommend no change, you must still name the single pattern you would watch next and what evidence would change your mind."
5. **Pipeline (non-prompt): capture impact** — burst-sample (or select highest-motion frames) around the club-ball frame in the extractor so the vision stage can score face/strike at least at low confidence. This is the single biggest ceiling on DIAGNOSTIC.
6. **Infra note (found during cleanup):** the production `golf-ai-analysis-processor` writes swing profiles to `golf-coach-swing-profiles-dev` (env `SWING_PROFILE_TABLE`); no non-suffixed prod table exists. Production user profiles landing in a `-dev` table looks unintentional and worth confirming.
