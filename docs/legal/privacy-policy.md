# Alki DivotLab Privacy Policy

Effective date: TBD

This Privacy Policy explains how Alki DivotLab ("Alki," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the Alki DivotLab mobile application and related services (the "Service").

## 1. Who We Are

Alki DivotLab is a product of Alki Golf Co. Alki DivotLab provides AI-powered golf swing coaching. Users can sign in, upload golf swing videos, receive AI-generated swing analysis, and ask follow-up coaching questions.

Contact: support@divotlab.ai

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated through your use of the Service, and limited information from service providers that help us operate the Service.

### Account Information

When you sign in, we may collect:

- Name, email address, and account identifiers from your sign-in provider.
- Authentication identifiers from providers such as Google, Apple, and Amazon Cognito.
- Basic account status, such as whether your account is active.

### Swing Videos and Analysis Data

When you upload a swing video, we may collect and process:

- The uploaded video file.
- Frames extracted from the video for analysis.
- Video metadata, such as upload time, file path, duration, frame count, and processing status.
- AI-generated swing observations, coaching summaries, scores, practice recommendations, and follow-up chat context.
- Analysis history used to compare progress over time.

### Chat and Support Messages

We collect messages you send to the AI coach and messages you send to support. Support emails may include account and app context, such as your email address, user ID, app environment, platform, RevenueCat customer ID, and entitlement status, so we can investigate issues.

### Subscription and Purchase Information

Purchases are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment card details. We may receive subscription-related information such as:

- Product purchased.
- Subscription status.
- Trial, renewal, cancellation, or expiration events.
- RevenueCat customer identifiers.
- App Store transaction or receipt metadata needed to manage access.

### Device, Usage, and Diagnostic Information

We may collect technical information needed to operate, secure, and improve the Service, such as:

- Device platform and app version.
- Log data, crash reports, error messages, and performance data.
- API request metadata and processing status.
- Approximate usage events, such as upload, analysis, chat, paywall, purchase, restore, or support actions.

We do not intend to collect precise location data, contacts, health records, or biometric identifiers.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create and maintain your account.
- Authenticate sign-in and protect account access.
- Upload, process, and analyze golf swing videos.
- Generate AI-powered swing feedback and follow-up coaching responses.
- Maintain chat continuity and swing history.
- Provide subscription access, free trials, purchase restoration, and entitlement checks.
- Respond to support requests.
- Debug failures, monitor performance, prevent abuse, and improve the Service.
- Comply with legal, tax, accounting, App Store, and platform obligations.

## 4. AI Processing

Alki DivotLab uses AI service providers, including OpenAI, to generate swing analysis and conversational coaching. To provide these features, we may send uploaded video frames, extracted observations, user questions, and related swing context to AI systems for processing.

AI-generated feedback may be incomplete, inaccurate, or not suitable for every person. Do not rely on Alki DivotLab as a substitute for professional coaching, medical advice, physical therapy, or injury prevention advice.

## 5. How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the Service, including:

- Cloud infrastructure providers, such as Amazon Web Services, for authentication, storage, APIs, databases, and processing.
- AI providers, such as OpenAI, for swing analysis and chat responses.
- Subscription infrastructure providers, such as RevenueCat, for entitlement and purchase management.
- App platform providers, such as Apple, for App Store purchases, subscription management, crash reporting, and platform compliance.
- Email or support tools used to receive and respond to support messages.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, to prevent fraud or abuse, or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use uploaded swing videos for third-party advertising.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Videos, frames, analysis results, chat history, and account data may be stored using cloud infrastructure. Access is limited to systems and personnel who need it to operate, debug, secure, or support the Service.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain your account, support subscriptions, improve reliability, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Uploaded videos, extracted frames, analysis results, and chat history may be retained while your account remains active so that the AI coach can reference your swing history and progress. We may retain limited records after account deletion when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, or App Store transaction reasons.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict certain personal information. You may also have the right to object to certain processing.

To request access, correction, deletion, or export of your data, contact support@divotlab.ai.

If the app supports account creation, App Store policy may require an in-app way to initiate account deletion. Until a fully automated deletion flow is available, you may initiate deletion by contacting support. We will explain the expected timeline and confirm completion when deletion is complete.

## 9. Subscriptions and Apple Account Management

You can manage or cancel App Store subscriptions through your Apple account settings. Restoring purchases in the app may require contacting Apple, RevenueCat, or our support team depending on the issue.

Deleting your Alki DivotLab account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. You must cancel subscriptions through Apple.

## 10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact support@divotlab.ai and we will take appropriate steps.

If a higher minimum age applies in your region, you may only use the Service if you meet that age requirement.

## 11. International Processing

We may process and store information in the United States and other locations where we or our service providers operate. Privacy laws in those locations may differ from the laws where you live.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice as appropriate, such as by updating the effective date, posting notice in the app, or using another reasonable method.
